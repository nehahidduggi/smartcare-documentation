Inside SmartCare Assist

A rule-based clinical decision-support module within Carecrypt (engine version 2.0.0). It supports preliminary risk assessment, symptom and vital analysis, maternal risk evaluation, escalation categories, confidence estimation and condition-group matching.

Rule-based assessment

Predefined symptom weights, vital-sign thresholds, symptom clusters and comorbidity weights. No machine learning is used.

Structured risk evaluation

Produces a capped 0–100 score, a Green/Amber/Red category, an urgency label and a list of reasons.

Explainable outputs

Returns reasons, a scoring log, confidence components and up to three matching condition groups.

SmartCare Assist is a decision-support tool, not a diagnostic system.

How the pipeline works

The order below is the order assess() in smartcare/index.js runs. Select a stage in the diagram or the cards.

  1. Maternal risk

    Detects pregnancy from medical history and adds age and blood-pressure modifiers.

    In
    age, symptoms, medicalHistory, vitals
    Out
    isMaternal, riskScoreModifier, reasons
    File
    engines/maternalRiskEngine.js

  2. Base risk

    Scores symptoms, severity, age, vitals, clusters, duration and comorbidities; matches a condition group.

    In
    all inputs plus isMaternal
    Out
    score, reasons, warnings, conditionCode, treatments, labTests, vitalFindings, reasoningLog
    File
    engines/riskEngine.js

  3. Escalation

    Combines base and maternal results, applies priority rules and the stability guard.

    In
    base, maternal, symptoms, severity, vitals, age
    Out
    riskColor, urgency, finalScore, suggestedActions, allReasons
    File
    engines/escalationEngine.js

  4. Confidence (DCI)

    Rates how complete and consistent the input is.

    In
    age, symptoms, vitals, base, maternal, escalation
    Out
    confidenceScore, confidenceLevel, dci, reasoning
    File
    engines/confidenceEngine.js

  5. Differential

    Returns the top three condition groups by symptom-pattern match.

    In
    symptoms
    Out
    [{ code, matchScore, urgency }]
    File
    engines/riskEngine.js

Risk engine

Rule-based evaluation in riskEngine.js: 14 condition groups, hand-set weights, capped at 100.

Inputs it scores

  • Severity: High 35, Medium 15, Low 5
  • Symptom weights (5–15 each, 8 if unknown, capped at 50 in total)
  • Age: under 2 +30, under 12 +10, 65+ +20, 75+ +35
  • Red flags: chest pain, breathlessness, confusion, severe bleeding, difficulty swallowing
  • Four symptom clusters, duration, comorbidities
  • Medication warnings from a six-drug table (a small list, not a full interaction checker)

Vital checks (implemented thresholds)

Software thresholds, not medical advice. Diastolic ranges are defined but analyzeVitals does not check them.

Separate from the pipeline: calculateWeightedRiskScore is exported but not called by assess(); it uses its own thresholds and assumes °F.

Escalation engine

Turns scores into a configured urgency category. These rules are not official triage guidelines.

Low · Green

Score below 35 with no override. Routine care and follow-up.

Medium · Amber

Score 35–69, or selected pediatric and maternal rules. Primary-care review.

High · Red

Score 70 or above, or severe symptoms. Emergency evaluation suggested.

  1. Score ≥100: Red, critical
  2. Severe symptoms (chest pain, breathing difficulty, seizures): seizures, confusion and difficulty swallowing are always Red; isolated chest pain in a young patient with stable vitals, or breathlessness with stable vitals, is Amber
  3. Age under 5 with fever and base score above 40: Amber
  4. Maternal modifier ≥25: Red, specialist referral
  5. Score bands: ≥70 High, ≥35 Medium
  6. Moderate maternal modifier: Amber
  7. Persistent moderate symptoms: Amber, but this rule cannot fire today (see notes)

Stability guard: a Red result becomes Amber unless a critical-trigger symptom is present (chest pain, breathlessness, confusion, severe bleeding, seizures, difficulty swallowing) or the score is above 85. This does not replace a clinician's judgment.

Diagnostic Certainty Index

A software confidence estimate. It is not a validated probability that a diagnosis is correct.

88
Data completeness · ×0.40
Symptom coherence · ×0.30
Vital alignment · ×0.20
Conflict penalty · −5 each, max −10

Fixed example from a synthetic input (fever, headache, vomiting; complete vitals). Levels: Low 0–40, Moderate 41–70, High 71–100. Because the weights sum to 0.9, the highest achievable score is 90.

Maternal risk assessment

Two exported functions in maternalRiskEngine.js. Only the first is used by the pipeline.

evaluateMaternalRisk()

Called by assess(). Pregnant if history contains “pregnancy” or “pregnant”.

  • Age under 18 or over 35: +15
  • Systolic ≥140 or diastolic ≥90: +30

calculateMaternalRiskScore()

Exported, but not called by the pipeline.

  • Age under 18 or over 35: +15
  • Systolic ≥140: +20 · Haemoglobin under 10: +20
  • Previous complications: +25 · Risk symptoms: +20
  • Levels: ≤30 normal, ≤60 moderate, above 60 high (capped at 100)

These functions implement software rules. Their outputs are not independent diagnoses or a replacement for antenatal care.

Known implementation notes

Found by reading and running the code. The original engines were not modified.

  • The DCI cannot exceed 90: its weights sum to 0.9.
  • Condition entries have a key but no name, so base.condition and differential names are undefined. The adapter works around this using conditionCode and labels.
  • The persistent-symptoms escalation rule reads duration and severity from the base result, which never returns them, so it never fires.
  • The stability guard can downgrade a maternal Red to Amber while urgency stays “Critical”.
  • The “Severe” multiplier (×1.20) is unreachable via the adapter, which only emits Low, Medium or High.
  • The engines are hand-weighted and not clinically validated (see SOURCE.md).

Decision support, not diagnosis

  • SmartCare is a rule-based module for preliminary risk assessment. It does not confirm medical conditions.
  • Outputs depend on the accuracy and completeness of the input.
  • Risk categories, confidence scores and condition matches must be interpreted by healthcare professionals.
  • It does not replace clinical examination, laboratory investigation or emergency services. Severe or urgent symptoms need appropriate medical attention.
